WHC PURCHASE AGREEMENT: DOCUPLETE HANDOFF

Owner-confirmed policies
- WHC accepts self-serve online and representative-assisted phone purchases.
- All non-IRA purchases require staffed FedEx Office or FedEx Ship Center pickup.
- For self-serve purchases, the agreement price becomes binding when the buyer signs.

Website and Git source status
The checkout preview, public policy pages, PDF generator, blank-template export,
and mapped two-page purchase-agreement package source have been aligned.
Sample, blank, and mapped review PDFs were generated without changing live data.
The separately stored live Docuplete package has NOT been replaced. A Git push
does not automatically prove that an existing package's stored PDF was updated.

Safe mapped-template export, with no database writes:
pnpm --filter @workspace/api-server exec tsx src/scripts/seedPurchaseAgreementPackage.ts --export-only

This writes the PDF and corresponding field/mapping export to the ignored
generated-artifacts/whc-review directory. Do not run the seeder without
--export-only for this review: its normal mode deletes/recreates a package.
When replacing a live document, keep its PDF and matching field coordinates
together; legal-text wrapping can move the signature block.

Exact replacement wording for the self-serve agreement's Price paragraph
For self-serve purchases, the price stated in your purchase agreement becomes
binding when you sign it. Payment funds the signed order; it does not set a new
price. Review the product quantities, unit prices, shipping, and total in the
agreement before signing. Shipment follows receipt of cleared funds.

Delivery paragraph to use consistently
All non-IRA purchases require pickup at a staffed FedEx Office or FedEx Ship
Center. We do not ship non-IRA orders to residential addresses or unmanned
drop boxes. Adult signature is required at pickup.

Signing-link distinction
Requesting or receiving a signing link does not make the purchase binding.
The purchase becomes binding when the buyer signs the agreement.

Keep the existing wire deadline and default fee unchanged unless separately
approved: payment received by end of the next business day after signing;
default fee of the greater of $125 or actual market loss.

Verification before release
1. Check the current Docuplete package used by WHC, including its base document
   and any generated clauses. Preserve all mapped fields and signature fields.
2. Remove any claim that self-serve pricing is first confirmed after funds clear
   or may vary at execution. Make sure the agreement and invoice use the same
   order quantities, unit prices, shipping, and total.
3. Check that delivery is staffed FedEx pickup, not residential delivery, and
   that no call is required for the self-serve order.
4. Review the agreement alongside WHC Terms and Disclosures. Have the contract
   wording reviewed before release; this handoff is not legal certification.
5. When explicitly authorized to resume end-to-end testing, use a designated
   test session to inspect the actual document, signature completion, invoice,
   and staff notification. Do not test against a real customer's order.

Scope limits
This update changes wording only. It does not add automated supplier trading,
price-lock enforcement, or new signing-session behavior. Staff must verify the
supplier lock and honor the price in the signed self-serve agreement.
